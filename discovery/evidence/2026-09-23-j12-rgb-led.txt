2026-09-23  J12 RGB LED: COMMON ANODE, DIFFUSED, FORWARD VOLTAGES
===================================================================
Closes: bringup A7.4
        capture-checklist.md "Measure the RGB forward voltages and
        confirm common anode", which had been TICKED without being done

ARTICLE
  5 mm 4-pin common-anode RGB, diffused, for J12.
  sourcing.md records CHANZON B01C19ENFK as the reference listing and
  then says "get the diffused version ... from AliExpress at the same
  specification". WHICH LISTING THIS CAME FROM IS NOT ESTABLISHED, and
  it turns out not to matter: THE BAG LABEL CARRIES THE SAME SPEC the
  repo already recorded, so the recorded figures do describe the part
  in hand whichever listing supplied it.

  BAG LABEL, read 2026-09-23:  R 2.0-2.2 V,  G/B 3.0-3.2 V

INSTRUMENTS
  Fluke 101; Jesverty supply; nominal 1k resistor MEASURED at 990 ohm.

A7.4  COMMON ANODE
------------------
  Diode test, RED PROBE ON THE COMMON PIN, conducts to the other three.
  => COMMON ANODE. PASS.
  This is what the board needs. The common-CATHODE version of the same
  product differs by four characters in the listing and would have
  needed a high-side switch per channel, twelve parts instead of three.

DIFFUSED
--------
  PASS, by eye. The status scheme depends on MIXED colours, amber as
  red plus green and magenta as red plus blue. Water-clear would show
  three coloured dots, which is not a state anyone reads.

FORWARD VOLTAGES
----------------
  supply --> 990 ohm --> die --> 0 V, Fluke across the die.

  die      Vf at 5 V    I         Vf at 3 V    I
  -----    ---------    -------   ---------    -------
  red       1.932 V     3.10 mA    1.849 V     1.16 mA
  green     2.454 V     2.57 mA    2.322 V     0.69 mA
  blue      2.607 V     2.42 mA    2.529 V     0.48 mA

  SANITY CHECK THAT THESE ARE REAL: red < green < blue, which is the
  bandgap ordering by photon energy. A transposed or mis-probed set
  would not come out in that order.

🔴 THESE DO NOT CONTRADICT THE 3.0-3.2 V LABEL
-----------------------------------------------
  An earlier reading of this data claimed they did, by fitting a
  LOGARITHMIC diode curve to the two low-current points and projecting
  8x out to the label's 20 mA condition. THAT WAS WRONG AND IS
  WITHDRAWN.

  A log-only fit OMITS SERIES RESISTANCE. An InGaN green or blue die
  carries 10-30 ohm of it, which contributes a LINEAR IR term that
  dominates at 20 mA and is invisible at 2 mA. Restore 20 ohm and
  2.454 V at 2.57 mA is entirely consistent with 3.0-3.2 V at 20 mA.

  THE CLAIMED VALIDATION WAS ALSO WRONG. The argument ran: red landed
  inside the vendor band, therefore the method is sound. IT IS SOUND
  FOR RED ONLY. Red is AlGaInP with low series resistance and a flatter
  curve, which is exactly why a log-only fit works there and fails on
  the other two. A check that only passes on the case never in doubt
  is not a check.

  => THE LABEL IS PROBABLY RIGHT. Settle it, if it is ever worth
     settling, by measuring AT 20 mA: supply in CC at 20 mA straight
     into the die, no resistor, read Vf. No model, no extrapolation.

  IT DOES NOT MATTER FOR J12. The RGB runs from the 5 V rail with
  headroom either way. It mattered for J4, which hangs on VOUT, and
  that was settled there by measuring the operating point directly.

WHAT THE READINGS DO SHOW: A REAL DEFECT IN values.md
------------------------------------------------------
  values.md, "changed: the RGB cannot be driven from a 3V3 GPIO",
  computes:

      channel   Vf used   headroom   through 330 ohm
      red       ~2.0 V    +1.15 V    3.5 mA
      green     ~3.0 V    +0.15 V    0.45 mA     "invisible"
      blue      ~3.1 V    +0.05 V    0.15 mA     "invisible"

  🔴 IT USES A 20 mA FORWARD VOLTAGE AT A MILLIAMP OPERATING POINT.
  That is the SAME error as the withdrawn extrapolation above, pointing
  the other way. At the 1-3 mA such a circuit would actually run, green
  measures ~2.3-2.45 V, not 3.0.

  Reworked at the real operating point, iterating to convergence:

      green   headroom ~0.85 V   ->  about 2.2 mA
      blue    headroom ~0.65 V   ->  about 1.7 mA

  Dim, but NOT "invisible", and NOT "no resistor value fixes it":
  100-150 ohm would give 4-5 mA.

  🔴 THE DECISION STILL STANDS AND SHOULD NOT BE REVISITED. 5 V common
  anode with the GPIOs SINKING is better regardless: an STM32 sinks
  harder than it sources, the 5 V rail is stiffer than a pin's
  output-high, and the currents are properly defined. The 3V3 route
  would have worked with far less margin.

  WHAT IS WRONG IS THE STATED REASON, which is written as physics
  rather than as a property of one part at one current. If the LED is
  ever swapped, someone will rely on it.

THE BOARD CURRENTS SURVIVE
---------------------------
  An earlier claim that green and blue would run ~30% over their design
  current was an artefact of the same bad extrapolation. WITHDRAWN.
  With the label's forward voltages, values.md's existing 5.9 / 6.7 /
  6.3 mA per die stand.

  ⚠️ BUT values.md AND sourcing.md DISAGREE WITH EACH OTHER, and did
  before any of this:

      values.md     5.9 / 6.7 / 6.3 mA     low-end Vf, NO sink drop
      sourcing.md   4.80-5.20 / 4.83-5.50  mid Vf, WITH 0.35 V drop

  Both are defensible arithmetic; they differ on whether to allow for
  the GPIO's output-low drop. Neither is wrong, and the project should
  pick one. Not resolved here, flagged.
