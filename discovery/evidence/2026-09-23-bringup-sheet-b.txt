            TWO CORRECTIONS, IN ORDER, BECAUSE BOTH ARE INSTRUCTIVE.

            1. As first taken it looked like a 3-2 split, b1/b5 at ~195k against
               b2/b3/b4 at 0.58M, and was noted as probably curve position.
               b1 RETESTED ON CLIPS, with Dupont connectors on the pins: 0.59M.
               THE 195k WAS HAND-HELD PROBE CONTACT. At megohms the body sits in
               PARALLEL and pulls the reading DOWN, the opposite direction from
               the hook switch's 27k, so NEITHER DIRECTION OF ERROR IS
               REASSURING. Use clips. See A8.

            2. b5 was predicted to be the same artifact. IT IS NOT.
               b5 RETESTED ON CLIPS: STILL 190k. So the split is 4-1 and real:
               b5 is genuinely ~3x lower than the other four.

            WHAT b5 MIGHT BE: a ~280k parallel path would do it, which is
            leakage scale. A cap on VOUT, or part variation in the bq24074 OUT
            or U2 VIN pin. OR it may STILL be curve position, since reproducible
            on b5 is not the same as compared like-for-like against the others.

            🔴 NOT RESOLVED, AND DELIBERATELY NOT CHASED. Five orders of
            magnitude from any threshold and nothing downstream depends on it.
            IT IS THE FIRST THING TO LOOK AT IF BOARD 5 MISBEHAVES IN PHASE C.
            Free discriminator: watch whether b5 runs low on B2.4-B2.7 too.
                low on several rails  -> a board-level property
                low only on VOUT      -> specific to that net
                not low elsewhere     -> it was curve position after all
