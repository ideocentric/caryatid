CORRECTION, appended 2026-08-24 22:40 (evidence is append-only)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  The "check_schematic.py  0 hard findings, 8 advisory" line above is WRONG
  in both halves, and the way it was wrong is worth recording.

  The count was produced by a regex matching only `[near ] advisory`. There
  were 28 advisories, not 8: 8 near-misses plus 20 text overlaps that the
  regex never saw. Counting one class and reporting it as the total.

  Worse, the 20 were not advisory at all. Rendered at 600 dpi:

      power.kicad_sch     VIN_DC and PWR_FLAG share an anchor EXACTLY at
                          (69.85, 272.59) and print as illegible mush.
                          Same for GND, VBAT and +5V against PWR_FLAG.
      panel-io.kicad_sch  +3V3 prints straight through D12 on J13's 2.54 mm
                          pin pitch; each power flag's Value text is drawn
                          3.0 mm below its own symbol, landing it on the
                          NEXT PIN ROW DOWN. 15 instances of this shape.
      seed.kicad_sch      the PGOOD_LEG label bridge names both sides of the
                          R16/R17 divider midpoint; the two labels collide.

  They were dismissed because the tool hedged every text overlap as
  "advisory: character widths are estimated". CHAR_W is worth 0.1-0.2 mm on
  a short string. These run 0.81 to 1.27 mm.

  A confidence caveat is not a severity. The tool now grades by
  interpenetration depth: >= 0.60 mm is [collision] and counts as a finding.
  It reports 20.

  NO ELECTRICAL DEFECT IS IMPLIED. All 20 are text placement. The PGOOD_LEG
  bridge was traced to be sure: ~{PGOOD} -> R17 -> PGOOD_LEG -> R16 ->
  A11_DIV, a summing-ladder leg feeding one ADC pin, matching the *_LEG
  convention used for LEG_101_L and LEG_256_R. Netlist unchanged, 113 nets.
