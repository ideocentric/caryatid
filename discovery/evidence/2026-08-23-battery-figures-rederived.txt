then re-run. That is what makes the new figures checkable.

CHARGE = CC to ~80% of capacity, then the CV tail
  at 3000: CC 2.40 h + CV 1.10 h = 3.50 h   printed 3.5
  at 3400: CC 2.72 h + CV 1.25 h = 3.97 h
  at 3400, 1.5 A: 2.72 h   (printed 2.4 h at 3000)

RATE
  1.0 A on 3400 mAh = 0.294C   (was 0.33C)
  1.5 A on 3400 mAh = 0.441C   (was 0.50C)

Both models reproduce the printed 3000 mAh figures to within rounding,
so the 3400 mAh restatement rests on the same reasoning, not a scale factor.
