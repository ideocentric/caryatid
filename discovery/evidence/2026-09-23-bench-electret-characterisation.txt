TEST A: DC RESISTANCE, BOTH DIRECTIONS
---------------------------------------
  Capsule in free air, meter only. Readings on the 6 k range, so KILOHMS
  (three decimals is that range's signature).

  capsule            A1        A2        ratio    verdict
  ---------------    -------   -------   ------   ------------------------
  small, w/ leads    1.397 k   1.154 k   1.21     electret, integral FET
  large, uxcell      1.286 k   0.828 k   1.55     electret, integral FET
  (handset, shape)   1.76 k    1.05 k    1.68     electret, integral FET

  A1 on the small one is METER RED ON CAPSULE RED.

  ⚠️ THE LARGE CAPSULE'S READINGS ARE UNATTRIBUTED. It has no colour and no
  longer lead, so 1.286 and 0.828 are recorded WITHOUT knowing which pad
  the red probe was on. See the can-bond item below, which would fix it.

  BOTH PASS. Finite kilohms, clearly asymmetric. The asymmetry is the
  built-in JFET and it settles the type on its own, without depending on
  the magnitudes landing in any expected band. Neither is dynamic (steady,
  150-600 ohm) nor carbon (jumps when tapped).

TEST B: BIAS IT. SMALL CAPSULE, RED AT NODE B
----------------------------------------------
  3.30 V CV through 2174 ohm. This is R51's circuit exactly.

      B1  drain, B to C      2.558 V
      B2  across R, A to B   0.738 V   ->  339 uA
      B3  total,   A to C    3.301 V   (CV held)

  >>> RED IS POSITIVE. 339 uA at a 2.558 V drain.

  🔴 ONE READING SETTLES IT, AND THE REVERSE IS NOT NEEDED. A
  FORWARD-BIASED JUNCTION AT 339 uA WOULD SIT NEAR 0.6 V, NOT 2.558 V.
  There is no reading of this but a correctly biased JFET. The handset
  needed its reverse taken because the failure mode there was a 0.683 V
  junction drop that had to be SEEN to be believed; here the working
  orientation was hit first and is unambiguous on its own.

  TEST A PREDICTED IT, AND THAT IS NOW TWO FOR TWO:

      part             meter red on   higher reading   positive
      handset          yellow         1.798 k          yellow  ✅
      bench, small     red            1.397 k          red     ✅

  So on these two parts THE METER'S RED PROBE ON THE POSITIVE TERMINAL
  GIVES THE HIGHER RESISTANCE, each confirmed functionally afterwards.
  TWO SAMPLES IS NOT A LAW. It is a prior that has paid off twice, and
  the large capsule would be a third test of it.

  them, and the other sits inside an isolating gap as the drain.

  legged one should read near 0 ohm and the other open. Ten seconds, and

  PREDICTION IF DONE: meter red on the NON-LEGGED pad gives the HIGHER

  NOT BIASED, DELIBERATELY. It needs leads soldered before it is usable,
  and the small capsule is the better reference.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH
