===========================================================================
STAGE 1: DEAD-BOARD ELECTRICAL
===========================================================================

  Meter in RESISTANCE. Still no power.

  🔴 READ THIS BEFORE THE FIRST MEASUREMENT
  -----------------------------------------
  THE BULK CAPACITORS WILL FOOL YOU. C1, C2, C3, C4, C5, C6 are 10 to 22 uF
  and C7 is 100 uF. The meter's own test current charges them, so a rail-to-
  ground reading STARTS LOW AND CLIMBS. That looks exactly like a short.

      WAIT FOR THE READING TO SETTLE. A real short sits at its value and does
      not move. A capacitor climbs, and keeps climbing.

      Between measurements, short the rail to ground briefly to discharge it,
      or the next reading starts from wherever the last one left it.

  PROBE AT CONNECTOR PINS, not at fine-pitch parts. Every rail reaches a
  connector, and THE BOARD SILKSCREENS ALL 77 CONNECTOR PIN NAMES. Read the
  board. Do not work from memory, and do not work from this sheet where the
  board itself will tell you.


1a   RAIL TO GROUND
     -------------
     Efficient order: probe every rail ONE WAY first (red on rail, black on
     GND). A high settled reading is a pass and needs nothing more. ONLY where
     it reads low do you reverse the probes, because that is the only case
     where the answer matters.

       low one way, high the other  -> a semiconductor junction. NORMAL.
       low BOTH ways                -> 🔴 SHORT. Stop on that board.

     rail      reach it at            b1        b2        b3        b4        b5

     VIN_DC    J1                     ......    ......    ......    ......    ......

     VOUT      J3, J4                 ......    ......    ......    ......    ......

1b   RAIL TO RAIL
     ------------
     A stray bridge between adjacent connector pins shows here and NOT in 1a,
     because both ends are live rails and neither is ground.

     pair                b1        b2        b3        b4        b5
     ----------------    ------    ------    ------    ------    ------
     +5V  <-> +3V3       ......    ......    ......    ......    ......
     +3V3 <-> +3V3A      ......    ......    ......    ......    ......
     +3V3A<-> +3V3D      ......    ......    ......    ......    ......
     VOUT <-> +5V        ......    ......    ......    ......    ......

     +3V3, +3V3A and +3V3D are SEPARATE NETS on this board and must not read
     as one. If they do, that is either a bridge or a misunderstanding of the
     board, and it is worth resolving before Stage 2 either way.


1c   GROUND CONTINUITY
     -----------------
     AGND and DGND are one net here. Both should be continuous with the main
     pour and with each other, near 0 ohm.

     J5 pin 10 <-> J11 pin 10     b1 ......  b2 ......  b3 ......  b4 ......  b5 ......

     An OPEN reading is as much a fault as a short: it means a pour or a via
     did not do its job, and the ground stitching was one of the last things
     done to this board.


1d   D1 ORIENTATION
     --------------
     Diode test across D1, the SS34 in band 1.

       forward   expect 0.2 to 0.4 V. A SCHOTTKY, LOWER than silicon's 0.6 V.
                 A reading near 0.6 to 0.7 V means it is not the part you
                 think it is.
       reverse   open

     b1 fwd ......  b2 fwd ......  b3 fwd ......  b4 fwd ......  b5 fwd ......

     Reversed, D1 blocks the input instead of protecting it and the board
     simply never powers. That failure is silent and looks like a dead board.
