WHY THE CHECKER MISSED IT, AND WHAT CHANGED
--------------------------------------------------------------
  check_schematic.py reported this sheet clean three times. Three causes:
    1. it compared the symbol ORIGIN to the frame, not its extent
    2. it skipped every ref starting with '#', so the GND flag 0.9 mm
       from the paper edge was never tested -- that filter was written
       for the label-crossing check and copied where it did not belong
    3. lib_extents() measures graphics only; Device:C's body half-height
       is 0.762 mm and its pins reach 3.81 mm

  Fixed by adding lib_reach() (body + pins) and testing the extent of
  every symbol including power flags. Regression-tested against the
  pre-fix tree, where it now reports:
      C21 at (144.78,407.67) crosses the frame, bottom by 1.48 mm
      GND at (144.78,416.56) crosses the frame, bottom by 9.10 mm
  Those match the hand-derived overshoots exactly.
