2026-09-23  J13 QWIIC CONNECTOR FACES INTO THE BOARD
======================================================
  Found by Matt during Phase B, B1 visual inspection, with the physical
  board in hand. Confirmed from the design file afterwards.

  🔴 NOTHING WAS CHANGED. Discovery observes; remediation is a separate
     deliberate task. The PCB, schematic and netlist are untouched.

THE OBSERVATION
---------------
  Matt, looking at a fabricated board: J13 faces the wrong direction. It
  will need rotating 180 degrees, with the silkscreen and the connections
  fixed to match, in a future revision.

CONFIRMED FROM hardware/pcb/caryatid.kicad_pcb
-----------------------------------------------
  Board outline (Edge.Cuts)   x 50.00 to 200.00,  y 30.00 to 120.00
                              150 x 90 mm

  J13    at x 170.50, y 37.00, rotation 0
  footprint  Connector_JST:JST_SH_SM04B-SRSS-TB_1x04-1MP_P1.00mm_Horizontal

  Footprint local geometry:
      signal pads 1-4     y = -2.00
      mounting posts MP   y = +1.88   (x = -2.80 and +2.80)
      courtyard           y = -3.28 to +3.28

  ON A RIGHT-ANGLE SH CONNECTOR THE CONTACT TAILS EXIT THE HOUSING ON THE
  SIDE OPPOSITE THE CABLE OPENING. With the signal pads at -Y, THE OPENING
  FACES +Y. At rotation 0 that is +Y on the board.

      J13 is 7.0 mm from the y=30 edge.
      The opening faces AWAY from that edge, into 83 mm of board.

  ⚠️ PROVENANCE OF THE TWO HALVES, KEPT SEPARATE. Matt's observation is a
  DIRECT READING OF A PHYSICAL PART and is the primary evidence. The file
  analysis is a geometric inference from pad and courtyard positions; it
  CORROBORATES the observation and supplies coordinates, and it is not
  what establishes the fault.

CURRENT NET ASSIGNMENT, which is correct Qwiic and must be preserved
---------------------------------------------------------------------
      pad 1   GND
      pad 2   +3V3
      pad 3   D12    I2C1_SDA
      pad 4   D11    I2C1_SCL

  That matches the Qwiic / STEMMA-QT standard. THE PINOUT IS NOT THE FAULT.

WHAT A FIX INVOLVES, FOR A FUTURE REVISION
-------------------------------------------
  1. Rotate J13 180 degrees.
  2. 🔴 THE PAD ORDER REVERSES. Physical positions 1-2-3-4 become 4-3-2-1,
     so the ROUTING MUST BE REWORKED to keep GND / +3V3 / SDA / SCL landing
     on the same physical contacts. Rotating alone silently mirrors the
     Qwiic pinout, which is worse than the present fault: a reversed Qwiic
     cable puts +3V3 on GND.
  3. Move the pin-1 silkscreen marker.
  4. RE-CHECK CLEARANCE to the y=30 edge after rotating. The opening would
     then face an edge 7 mm away, and the courtyard already reaches 3.28 mm.
     Confirm the mating cable's strain relief clears the edge and the
     enclosure wall.

IMPACT
------
  🔴 IT DOES NOT GATE THIS BUILD. loa does not use J13. Matt's own words:
  "The current project is not expecting to use this."

  E10.1 ("I2C on J13 with a Qwiic device") IS STILL RUNNABLE. The cable
  routes awkwardly across the board; it does not prevent enumeration.

  It matters for any FUTURE consumer that wants the Qwiic port, and
  panel-io-sheet.md already lists a distance sensor against J13.

ROOT CAUSE, AND A GAP IN THE CHECKLIST THAT WOULD NOT HAVE CAUGHT IT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  sourcing.md records this footprint as REUSED FROM ABSONUS.

  capture-checklist.md carries, STILL UNTICKED:
      "Reuse the absonus footprints listed in sourcing.md - they are drawn
       and fabricated already. Check pin-1 orientation and JST polarity
       against THIS schematic; proven to fabricate is not proven correct
       here."

  THE WARNING WAS RIGHT IN SPIRIT AND TOO NARROW IN LETTER. It asks about
  PIN-1 ORIENTATION AND POLARITY, which are electrical. This fault is
  PHYSICAL FACING DIRECTION, and the pinout is correct. So the item as
  written would NOT have caught it even if it had been ticked.

  ⇒ THE CHECKLIST ITEM SHOULD BE BROADENED: a reused footprint must be
  checked for WHICH WAY IT FACES IN THE NEW BOARD, not only for pinout.
  A right-angle connector carries an orientation that a vertical one does
  not, and it is the only connector on this board where rotation changes
  anything but pin order.
