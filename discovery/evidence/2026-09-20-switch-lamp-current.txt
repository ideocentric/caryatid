2026-09-20  SWITCH LAMP CURRENT  (bringup A7.6)
================================================================
Closes: panel-latch-switch / still_open_but_NOT_a_compatibility_question
        values.md 5 V budget, "Switch lamp ... unmeasured / unknown"

ARTICLE
  12 mm illuminated latching pushbutton, ring LED, body marked 3-6V/W.
  The unit confirmed compatible with J3 on 2026-09-06.
  Lamp pair only. Switch contacts not in circuit for this measurement.
  NO BOARD INVOLVED. Supply, shunt, meter, switch.

INSTRUMENTS                                 (see bench-instruments.yaml)
  Meter     Fluke 101, DC mV range. No current ranges, hence the shunt.
  Supply    Jesverty 0-30 V 0-5 A, CC, limit 100 mA.
  Shunt     10.2 ohm, measured on the Fluke before use.

  !! The shunt was measured 2-WIRE and the LEAD RESISTANCE WAS NOT
     SUBTRACTED. Typical clip leads are 0.1-0.3 ohm, so the true shunt
     may be nearer 10.0 ohm and every current below may read up to
     ~2% LOW. At the 5.00 V point that is 28.5 mA vs up to 29.1 mA.
     Not corrected because it does not change any conclusion. Subtract
     the leads if this figure is ever needed to better than 2%.

METHOD
  Supply + -> shunt -> lamp "+" -> lamp "-" -> supply -.
  Fluke ACROSS the shunt, DC mV.  mA = mV / 10.2
  Lamp volts = supply setpoint - shunt drop. The shunt drop is NOT
  negligible at this current: at the 5.00 V setpoint it is 258 mV, so
  the lamp saw 4.74 V and not 5.00 V. That is why the third reading
  exists.

READINGS
  supply     shunt mV    current     volts across the lamp
  --------   ---------   ---------   ---------------------
  3.00 V      51.5        5.05 mA     2.949 V
  5.00 V     257.9       25.28 mA     4.742 V
  5.29 V     290.5       28.48 mA     5.000 V   <-- IN SERVICE

  The 5.29 V setpoint was chosen so that the drop across the shunt
  would leave exactly 5.00 V on the lamp. 5.29 - 0.2905 = 4.9995 V.

RESULT
  28.5 mA at 5.00 V. This is the figure for the 5 V budget, because
  R5 is a 0 ohm link and the lamp sees the full rail in service.
  5.6 mA at 3.00 V for the bottom of the marked range.

WHAT THE THREE POINTS SHOW
  They fit a straight line, so the "internal limiting" implied by a
  voltage-range marking is A PLAIN SERIES RESISTOR, not a constant
  current driver:

        Vf ~ 2.51 V,  R_series ~ 87.5 ohm

  measured    model     error
   5.05 mA     5.06      +0.2%
  25.28 mA    25.54      +1.0%
  28.48 mA    28.19      -1.0%

  The 1% spread is the LED's own Vf rising with current, which is what
  a real diode does. The agreement is the point: two-point and
  three-point fits give the same intercept to three figures.

  CONSEQUENCE. The current is NOT held across the 3-6 V marking, it
  tracks the rail almost 8:1 end to end:
        3.00 V ->  5.6 mA
        5.00 V -> 28.5 mA      (measured)
        6.00 V -> 39.9 mA      (extrapolated, top of the marking)
  So the budget line is only meaningful quoted against a rail voltage.

CONFIRMS A PREDICTION IN indicators.md
  That document said, conditionally, "if the limiting is a plain series
  resistor, roughly 75% of the current [at 5 V against a 6 V 4xAA
  reference], which the eye reads as around 85% as bright".
  IT IS a plain series resistor, and the ratio is 28.5/39.9 = 71%.
  The estimate was right and slightly optimistic. The brightness
  conclusion stands: no action, and no board change.
