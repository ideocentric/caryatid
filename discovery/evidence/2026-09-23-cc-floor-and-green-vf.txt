TEST 1: THE SUPPLY'S CC IS OFFSET, NOT SCALED
==============================================
  10.2 ohm shunt in series with the RGB green. Supply in CC.

  set      A-B mV    real mA     excess
  ------   -------   ---------   ------
  2 mA      61        5.98       +3.98
  5 mA      87        8.53       +3.53
  10 mA    138       13.53       +3.53

  >>> real = set + 3.53 mA.  UNITY GAIN, FIXED OFFSET.

  The 5 and 10 mA pair gives a slope of EXACTLY 1.000, which kills the
  competing offset-plus-gain model. Two consequences, both permanent:

  🔴 THE SUPPLY CANNOT DELIVER LESS THAN ~3.5 mA IN CC, whatever is set.
     That is the floor bench-instruments has wanted since the supply
     arrived. It is a property of the instrument, not a resolution limit.

  🔴 TO GET A CURRENT, SET IT 3.5 mA LOW. 20 mA wanted -> set 16.5.

  The 2 mA point runs +0.45 mA above the model, and the current was
  visibly wandering 5.2-6.8 mA there. Regulation is WORSE at the bottom
  as well as offset, so the floor is soft rather than sharp.

  WHAT THIS EXPLAINS. On 2026-09-22 the same die read 2.54-2.59 V with
  the supply set to 2 mA, against 2.454 V at 2.57 mA under constant
  voltage: voltage UP as current DOWN, which a diode cannot do. It was
  never at 2 mA. It was at about 6 mA. ONE LED, NO UNIT VARIATION, and
  nothing wrong with either reading. The supply was lying.

🔴 THIS RESOLVES THE values.md / sourcing.md DISAGREEMENT
=========================================================
  Both documents give per-die currents for J12 and they disagree. The
  cause is now clear: BOTH USED THE LABEL'S 20 mA Vf AT A ~6 mA
  OPERATING POINT. values.md then omitted the GPIO's 0.35 V sink drop,
  and on green and blue that second error CANCELLED the first.

  Recomputed from measured forward voltages at the current each channel
  actually converges to, with the 0.35 V sink drop included:

      die     measured-based   values.md   sourcing.md
      red        ~5.2 mA        5.9  ✗      4.80-5.20  ✓
      green      ~6.8 mA        6.7  ✓      4.83-5.50  ✗
      blue       ~6.4 mA        6.3  ✓      4.83-5.50  ✗

  NEITHER DOCUMENT IS RIGHT ABOUT ALL THREE. values.md is right on green
  and blue by cancellation and high on red; sourcing.md is right on red
  and low on the other two.

  values.md's TOTALS survive well: amber 12.0 mA measured against 12.6
  stated, all three 18.4 against 18.9. Within 3-5%.

  THE CORRECT METHOD, and the durable point: USE THE FORWARD VOLTAGE AT
  THE CURRENT THE CIRCUIT ACTUALLY CONVERGES TO, which means iterating,
  because Vf and I determine each other. A datasheet Vf carries its test
  current with it and is not a constant.
