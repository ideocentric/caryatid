PART 1. THE FOUR CONDUCTORS, DC RESISTANCE BOTH WAYS
----------------------------------------------------
  Readings on the Fluke's 6.000 k range, so all values are KILOHMS.
  (Three decimals is that range's signature. The receiver figure of
  145.3 ohm recorded on 2026-08-25 has one decimal, which is the
  600 ohm range. Consistent both ways.)

  INNER PAIR, red and green
      red-red   / green-black     0.821 k
      red-black / green-red       0.821 k
      => IDENTICAL BOTH WAYS. Passive, no semiconductor.
         This is the RECEIVER pair.

  OUTER PAIR, yellow and black
      yellow-red   / black-black  1.798 k
      yellow-black / black-red    1.056 k
      => POLARITY DEPENDENT. A semiconductor junction.
         This is the TRANSMITTER pair, the electret.

  AGREES WITH THE RECORD, taken a month earlier at the jack:
      2026-08-25   inner 0.824 k      outer 1.054 k
      2026-09-22   inner 0.821 k      outer 1.056 k
      0.4% and 0.2% apart, two riggings, a month apart.

  WHAT IS NEW HERE:
   1. THE COLOURS. The record established "the outer pair is the
      transmitter" but never which conductors that meant. It is
      YELLOW and BLACK. The receiver is RED and GREEN.
   2. THE INNER PAIR IS SYMMETRIC. Only one direction was taken in
      2026-08-25, so nothing had ruled out a second semiconductor.
   3. The outer pair's HIGH-side direction (1.798 k) had never been
      measured at the jack, only at the capsule (1.75-1.77 k).

PART 2. POLARITY, BY BIASING IT  (the functional test)
-------------------------------------------------------
  Rig, which is R51's circuit exactly:

      3.3 V --> 2174 ohm --+--> capsule lead A
                           |
                      Fluke DC V
                           |
              capsule lead B --> 0 V

  A = YELLOW                           A = BLACK
      drain          2.882 V               drain          0.683 V
      across R       0.418 V               across R       2.617 V
      current        192.3 uA              current       1203.8 uA
      capsule as R   14.99 k               capsule as R    567 ohm

  >>> YELLOW IS POSITIVE (drain). BLACK IS NEGATIVE (source).

  WHY IT IS UNAMBIGUOUS, and not merely "one is lower":
    0.683 V AT 1.2 mA IS A SILICON JUNCTION FORWARD DROP. Reversed,
    the capsule is not operating at all, it is a forward-biased diode.
    192 uA meanwhile is textbook electret bias current, where the
    usual quoted range is 100-500 uA.

  IT AGREES WITH THE RESISTANCE TEST, independently. The red probe on
  YELLOW gave the HIGHER reading (1.798 k), which predicts yellow is
  the drain. Two methods, same answer.

  IT ALSO RECONCILES INTERNALLY: a junction's apparent resistance
  falls as current rises. 1.056 k at the meter's tiny test current,
  567 ohm at 1.2 mA. Same junction, two operating points.

PART 3. THE NUMBER THAT REPLACES A PLACEHOLDER
-----------------------------------------------
  OPERATING CURRENT = 192 uA per channel, at 3.3 V through 2k2.

  values.md budgets 1.5 mA per channel, which is 3.3 / 2200: the
  current with THE CAPSULE TREATED AS A SHORT. It is 7.8x TOO HIGH.
  The capsule is not a short, it presents about 15 k at its operating
  point, so the 2k2 is only 13% of the loop.

  For the pair, 384 uA rather than 3.0 mA on 3V3A.

  !! THE RECORD SAID THIS COULD NOT BE DONE ON THE BENCH.
     loa-handset-capsule, what_would_close_it: "Voltage across R51
     with the board powered ... Needs the boards, so it is not a bench
     item." THAT IS WRONG, and this rig is the disproof: 3.3 V through
     2k2 into the real capsule IS R51's circuit. No board required.

PART 4. A LEVER THAT IS NOW QUANTIFIED, FOR mic-gain-budget
-----------------------------------------------------------
  An electret's signal is developed ACROSS the load resistor, so the
  output scales with R51 while the JFET stays in saturation. Knowing
  the operating current tells you how far R51 can go:

      R51      drop      drain     signal vs 2k2
      2174     0.42 V    2.88 V    reference (as fitted)
      4k7      0.90 V    2.40 V    ~2.2x   (+6.7 dB)
      10k      1.92 V    1.38 V    ~4.6x   (+13.3 dB)
      12k      2.31 V    0.99 V    ~5.5x, drain getting tight

  SO THERE IS ROUGHLY 13 dB AVAILABLE from one 0603, if the gain
  budget ever turns out short. NOT A RECOMMENDATION: mic-gain-budget
  is still `unverified` and nothing says more gain is needed. It is
  recorded because it is only calculable now that the current is
  measured, and because it is cheaper than any other way of finding
  13 dB.

  CAVEAT: this assumes the JFET stays in saturation as the drain comes
  down. Supported at 2.88 V by this measurement; it would want
  re-measuring at the new operating point before being relied on.

  ALSO NOTED, a gap in mic-gain-budget rather than an error: its
  capsule_output_at_1pa range of -46 to -35 dBV/Pa carries NO LOAD
  CONDITION. Electret sensitivity is load-dependent, so a sensitivity
  figure without its load resistor and supply voltage is incomplete.

PART 5. OHMS ACCURACY, A BY-PRODUCT WORTH KEEPING
--------------------------------------------------
  A 1% 2k2 read 2174 ohm, so its true value is 2178-2222 and the meter
  reads somewhere between 0.2% and 2.2% LOW on the 6 k range. That is
  the FIRST ACTUAL ACCURACY CHECK on the Fluke's ohms function in this
  project. Lead resistance cannot explain it: leads add, and they
  measured 0.0 ohm.

  Consistent with the caveat already recorded against the switch lamp:
  if the meter reads low on ohms generally, the 10.2 ohm shunt is
  really a shade more and the lamp current a shade under 28.5 mA.
  Inside the few percent already stated. No figure moves.
